How the brain works

One shared neural network (about 12,000 numbers) reads the battle each turn: HP, speed, boosts, hazards, how hard each move hits, what each switch-in risks. It scores every legal move and switch. It started by copying Red's Grand Champion brain, then trained by playing itself, its past versions and every league brain. Wins pushed its choices up and losses pushed them down. It's the same idea as Jaxcalibur, at proof-of-concept scale: a few hundred thousand games on two CPU cores, against Jaxcalibur's ~100 million on a GPU, and no look-ahead search yet.
